1. CURRENT MODEL
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Location: models/best.pt
Size: 64 MB
Format: PyTorch .pt file (Ultralytics RT-DETR format)

Training Configuration:
  - Base Model: RT-DETR-L
  - Hardware: Kaggle 2x NVIDIA T4 GPUs
  - Epochs: 30
  - Batch Size: 32 (16 per GPU)
  - Optimizer: AdamW (lr0=0.0001, lrf=0.01)
  - Image Size: 640x640
  - Training Time: ~4 hours

Performance Metrics:
  - mAP@0.5: 0.792 (79.2%)
  - mAP@0.5:0.95: 0.547 (54.7%)
  - Precision: 0.812 (81.2%)
  - Recall: 0.768 (76.8%)
